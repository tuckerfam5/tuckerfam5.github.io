Bolt Bowl

7-on-7 robot football for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Bolt Bowl collects no personal data.

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your teams, save slots and settings are stored only on your device. We never see them. To delete them, delete the game from your device.
  • About once a week the game downloads a small public file of team results from GitHub. The request sends nothing about you or your games. Like any website, GitHub's servers see the internet address that asks for the file (see GitHub's privacy statement). Bolt Bowl never receives it.
  • Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to share, sell, keep or delete.
  • If this policy ever changes, this page will show the change and a new date.

Questions: boltbowl.support@gmail.com

Support

  • How to play: in the game, open HOW TO PLAY from the main menu.
  • Saves: the game has three save slots and saves by itself. If a save ever can't be read, the game keeps it aside instead of writing over it, and offers to restore the last good copy.
  • This week's matchups need the internet about once a week. Without it, the game uses the last results it has, and Pick Your Own always works.
  • Something wrong? Email boltbowl.support@gmail.com. Please say which device you use and what happened.

About

Bolt Bowl is a parody. Teams are fictional robots and are not connected to or approved by any sports league or team.
