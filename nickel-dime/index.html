Nickel & Dime

A private budget planner for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Nickel & Dime collects no personal data. There are no accounts of our own, no sign-in, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your budgets, entries or receipts.

Where your data is stored

  • On your devices. Everything you enter is stored in the app on your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Watch.
  • In your own iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your data between your devices through Apple's iCloud, in your private iCloud account. Apple stores and protects it under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it.
  • Without iCloud, your data stays only on the device where you entered it.

Shared budgets

You can choose to share a budget with other people through iCloud. Only the people you invite can see it. Everyone in a shared budget sees its entries, including the name of the person who added each one. You can stop sharing or leave a shared budget at any time in More → Budgets.

Receipts and Apple Intelligence

  • Receipt photos you add are stored with the entry, on your devices and in your iCloud.
  • Reading a receipt, suggesting a category and writing your monthly summary all happen on your device using Apple's Vision and Apple Intelligence. Your receipts and spending are never sent to us or anyone else for this.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose to photograph a receipt.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick are shared with the app.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on bill reminders. They're scheduled on your device.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: only if you turn on the app lock. Apple handles it; the app never receives biometric data.
  • Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Spotlight show or log information on your own device when you ask. Spotlight results are removed while the app lock is on.

You can change any permission in the Settings app.

Backups and exports

CSV exports and JSON backups are created on your device and go wherever you choose to send or save them.

Children

Nickel & Dime isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a budget in More → Budgets, or delete the app. To remove synced data from iCloud too, open Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete Nickel & Dime's data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, this page will show the change and a new date.

Support

  • Getting started: on first launch, set up your budget, or tap "Explore with Sample Data" to look around first.
  • Syncing between devices: sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, on each device. Changes usually appear within a minute while the app is open.
  • Sharing a budget: More → Budgets → Sharing → Share Budget. The other person needs an Apple Account with iCloud. Choose whether they can make changes or only view.
  • Bill reminders: edit a bill in the Plan tab and turn on a reminder. Mark it paid right from the notification.
  • Backups: More → Data & Backups makes a full backup, including receipt photos. Restoring creates a new budget and never overwrites an existing one.
  • On an iPad or Mac: ⌘N adds an entry.
  • Something wrong? Email boltbowl.support@gmail.com. Please say which device you use and what happened. Never send your budget data, account numbers or receipts.
