Bolt Slapshot

3-on-3 robot arcade hockey for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Bolt Slapshot collects no personal data.

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game never uses the internet. This season's matchups for THIS WEEK are built into the app.
  • Your settings, practice stars and best scores are stored only on your device. We never see them. To delete them, delete the game from your device.
  • Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to share, sell, keep or delete.
  • If this policy ever changes, this page will show the change and a new date.

Questions: boltbowl.support@gmail.com

Support

  • How to play: in the game, open HOW TO PLAY from the main menu. PRACTICE has short drills for skating, shooting, passing and defense.
  • On a Mac: arrows or WASD to skate. E or J to pass or poke, Space or K to shoot or check, Shift or L for turbo, Q or I to switch. A game controller works too.
  • Something wrong? Email boltbowl.support@gmail.com. Please say which device you use and what happened.

About

Bolt Slapshot is a parody. Teams are fictional robots and are not connected to or approved by any sports league or team.
